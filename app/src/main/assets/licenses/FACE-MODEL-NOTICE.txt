On-device face verification models
==================================

YuNet face detector:
- Source: https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_detection_yunet
- Model: face_detection_yunet_2023mar_int8bq.onnx
- License: MIT; see YUNET-MIT-LICENSE.txt

SFace face recognizer:
- Source: https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_recognition_sface
- Model: face_recognition_sface_2021dec_int8bq.onnx
- License: Apache-2.0; see SFACE-APACHE-2.0-LICENSE.txt

The model repository's license notices do not establish the training-data
provenance or consent basis for this checkpoint. This integration is an
experimental prototype, not approved for production biometric use.
It does not implement liveness or presentation-attack detection. Do not rely
on a match as the sole child-safety or boarding control.
